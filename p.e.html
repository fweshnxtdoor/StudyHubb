Physical Education

Chapter 1: Types of Exercises

Unit I. Aerobic Exercises

  • Purpose: Improves cardiovascular endurance (heart and lungs).
  • Examples: Running, swimming, cycling.

Unit II. Strength Exercises

  • Purpose: Builds muscles and improves endurance.
  • Examples: Weightlifting, push-ups, squats.

Unit III. Flexibility Exercises

  • Purpose: Increases the range of motion of joints and muscles.
  • Examples: Stretching, yoga.

Unit IV. Balance Exercises

  • Purpose: Improves stability and coordination.
  • Examples: Tai chi, standing on one foot.

Chapter 2: Dynamic and Static Exercises

Unit I. Dynamic Exercises

  • Definition: Stretching with movement.
  • Purpose: To warm up muscles before physical activity.
  • Examples: Arm circles, walking lunges, high knees.

Unit II. Static Exercises

  • Definition: Stretching without movement, holding a position.
  • Purpose: To cool down muscles after physical activity.
  • Examples: Toe touch, butterfly stretch, quad stretch.

Unit III. When to Use

  • Dynamic Exercises → Before activities (for warm-up).
  • Static Exercises → After activities (for cool-down).

Chapter 3: Badminton

Unit I. Introduction to Badminton

  • Description: A racquet sport using a shuttlecock and net.
  • Players: Singles (1v1) or Doubles (2v2).

Unit II. Basic Rules

  • Serve diagonally into the opponent’s service box.
  • Only one hit allowed per side.
  • First to 21 points wins (must lead by 2 points).

Unit III. Basic Skills

  • Grip: Shake-hand style grip.
  • Serve: Underhand serve below the waist.
  • Clear: High and deep shot to the opponent’s backcourt.
  • Drop Shot: Light, short shot close to the net.
  • Smash: Powerful, downward attacking shot.

Unit IV. Key Techniques

  • Footwork: Stay light on your feet for quick movement.
  • Positioning: Stay in the center of your court.
